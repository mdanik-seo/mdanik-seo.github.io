Patients search by condition, not only by city. These terms are easy wins for the clinic's condition pages.

Quick wins found and fixed

The first audit found simple issues holding a great clinic back.

Right city signals
The homepage title targeted Atlanta, not Sandy Springs where the clinic is. Titles now target Sandy Springs and nearby areas.
Consistent business info
The address was written three different ways and the hours did not match. Name, address, phone and hours are now the same everywhere.
Updated profile
Old doctor information on the Google Business Profile was updated, and categories and services were cleaned up.
Duplicate page titles
Duplicate titles across service and condition pages were replaced with unique, keyword-focused titles.

What I did

One plan across the website and Google Maps.

Keyword strategy
Researched 200 local keywords and mapped primary and secondary keywords to 20 existing pages.
On-page SEO
Wrote new meta titles and meta descriptions for every page, based on the keyword map.
Schema markup
Added structured data so Google and AI search understand the clinic, its services and its location.
Google Business Profile
Mapped the main category and 12 services to target keywords, rewrote the business description and started weekly posts with a Book button.
Review system
Set up guidance so new reviews mention the service received and Sandy Springs, which helps Maps rankings.
Content plan
Planned 10 new pages (a Neck Pain page and 9 nearby-area pages) and 25 patient-education blog topics.
