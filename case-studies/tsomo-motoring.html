What I did

I owned the full SEO strategy from day one.

Technical SEO
Crawled the site with Screaming Frog, fixed broken links, improved site speed, and optimised the XML sitemap and robots.txt.
On-page SEO
Optimised title tags, meta descriptions, H1 to H3 structure, image alt text and internal linking.
Local SEO
Set up and optimised the Google Business Profile, fixed NAP consistency and built local citations for the Teesside area.
Link building
Earned quality backlinks from local directories, driving school listings and relevant UK sites.
Content strategy
Created locally targeted landing pages for Teesside-area driving lesson searches.
Analytics
Configured Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console for tracking and reporting.

Google Business Profile

Local SEO was the core of the strategy. I fully optimised the profile, including categories, services, photos, Q&A, posts and review responses, which brought consistent map views and search views from people nearby.
